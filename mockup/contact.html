Chat output of /hf contact

Type the command below and press Enter, or click Run.
Notes: WoW addons cannot open a mail app or a link, and players cannot copy plain FontString text. The email therefore sits in a single-line EditBox that is read-only in effect: an OnTextChanged handler resets it to the address (try typing in the box, it reverts), and OnEditFocusGained / OnMouseUp call HighlightText() so Cmd/Ctrl+C copies it. The Copy button only focuses and selects the box: addons cannot write to the clipboard. The email is never sent anywhere automatically; players copy it themselves. The "Report a problem" window reuses the existing /hf copy window (multi-line EditBox in a ScrollFrame). Client info comes from GetBuildInfo(), UnitClass("player") and GetAddOnMetadata; the sample text below is fake.
