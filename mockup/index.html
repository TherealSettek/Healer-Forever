WoW Forever API notes

WoW Forever runs on the modern (Midnight-era) addon API, so a few things work differently from old Classic addons.

✅ Fully supported: custom party frames, class colours, moving and resizing, minimap button, click-casting through secure buttons, health and power bars, hiding Blizzard party frames, aura icons with countdowns.
⚠️ Restricted in combat ("secret values"): during combat the addon can display health, heals and auras through Blizzard widgets but cannot calculate with them. So:
• Heal prediction and overheal use Blizzard's heal-prediction calculator, not addon arithmetic.
• Missing-buff buttons are reliable out of combat; in combat they freeze or hide.
• "Only dispellable debuffs" uses Blizzard's built-in dispel filter.
• Aura timers are drawn by Blizzard's duration/cooldown widgets.
• ⌘ (Cmd) as a click modifier must be verified in-game; ⌥ (Option) maps to Alt.
