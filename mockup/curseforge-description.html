Healer Forever

Party frames made for healing. See your group's health, mana, range and debuffs at a glance, heal with a mouse click, and get reminded when someone is missing a buff.

A 5-player party: heal prediction, a missing-blessing button, dispel colours, out of range and a dead player.

Party frames

  • Frames for you, your party and pets: horizontal (pets below) or vertical (pets to the right), movable as a group.
  • Class-coloured health bars with a power bar, heal prediction and overheal, and a border style you can adjust.
  • Range at a glance: OUT OF RANGE and NOT VISIBLE badges, a green edge on players you can heal, and CAN'T RESURRECT on dead players who are out of reach.
  • Target glow, aggro border, dead / ghost / offline states and an optional low-health pulse.

Frames & Bars options. Changes show on the frames straight away.

Click-casting

  • Bind spells to mouse buttons 1–8 with Shift, Ctrl, Alt (Option) and Cmd on Mac.
  • Casts only ever land on the player you clicked. If they are out of range or sight, nothing is cast, and nothing falls back onto you.
  • Clicking a dead player casts your class's resurrection spell.
  • The bar tooltip lists only the bindings for the modifier keys you are holding.

Click-casting: a spell for each mouse button and modifier.

Buff reminders

  • Pick buffs per class and role (Tank / Healer / DPS) from your own class's buffs: Paladin blessings, Priest Fortitude / Spirit / Shadow Protection, Mage Arcane Intellect with Dampen or Amplify Magic, Druid Mark of the Wild / Thorns.
  • A button appears on a player who is missing a buff, or whose buff is running out (default: under 5% left). Left-click casts the buff; right-click casts the group version.
  • Role buffs cover special cases, such as a tank-only buff.
  • Smart buff and Tank buff keybinds: one key press casts the next missing buff (one cast per press).

Buff presets: which of your buffs each class and role should get.

Buffs and debuffs

  • Buffs and debuffs are drawn by the game itself, so they stay accurate in combat and dispelled debuffs disappear at once.
  • Dispel-type colours, timers, adjustable positions, sizes and icons per row, and the game's own tooltip when you hover an icon.

Buffs & Debuffs options.

Minimap

  • A minimap button with status badges: missing buffs, the lock state and your active preset.
  • Party members shown as class-coloured orbs, with an X marking where a player died.

Minimap options.

Help built in

  • Help & Contact tab with all commands, quick start tips, and a Report a problem button that collects everything needed for a bug report, ready to copy.
  • Type /hf to open the options.

Notes

  • Made for WoW Forever.
  • Settings are saved per character.
  • Nothing is cast automatically: every cast is your own click or key press.
  • Addons can't detect line of sight to friendly players.

Problems or ideas? Use Help & Contact → Report a problem in game, or write to healer.forever.trtg@gmail.com.
