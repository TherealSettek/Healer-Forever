Matching minimap orbs

What the WoW API gives us: range (UnitInRange, IsSpellInRange / C_Spell.IsSpellInRange for a specific heal) and visibility (UnitIsVisible, false when the unit is too far away or in another phase). WoW does not expose line of sight to friendly units, so "behind a wall" cannot be detected before casting. A unit can show as in range and still fail the cast with "Target not in line of sight".
